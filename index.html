Who We Are

A Craft Passed
Down the Family

Prime Time Pool Plastering started the way the best trades do — at a father's side. What began as a skill handed down from one generation to the next has become a family-owned business built on the same care, the same standards, and the same pride in a job done right.

We specialize in pool plastering, resurfacing and remodeling for homeowners across Riverside and the Inland Empire. From a tired, stained surface to a brand-new finish you'll want to dive into — we treat every pool like it's our own.

  • Hands-on, owner-run — you work directly with us
  • Premium finishes — quartz, pebble & classic plaster
  • Honest quotes, clean work, no surprises
